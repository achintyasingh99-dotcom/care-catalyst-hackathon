Privacy & limitations

Understand what this prototype does and does not do before entering any information.

Where is data stored?

When you sign in, record lists and changes are sent to the local Python API and stored in its SQLite database. The browser also keeps a local view cache in localStorage. This prototype runs on your own computer by default and does not provide cloud synchronization. The SQLite database is not encrypted by this app, local browser data may remain on a shared device, and there is no secure backup or account recovery. Do not enter real patient information.

Is this a medical or diagnostic tool?

No. Care Catalyst organizes user-provided information and supports appointment preparation. It does not diagnose, rule out disease, prescribe treatment, or determine overall health. A lack of warnings does not mean that symptoms are safe.

Does it send reminders?

Follow-up dates are displayed inside the app. No email, SMS, push notifications, or background reminders are sent.

Can records be shared?

Nothing is automatically sent to a clinician or third party by this local prototype. Records are stored in the local database for the signed-in account. Printing a visit brief is a user-initiated action; review it before sharing. Signing out clears this app's browser cache keys and ends the local session, but it does not delete the account's records from the local database.

Use synthetic data

Use fictional demonstration records only. This prototype has not undergone a formal clinical, security, legal, or regulatory review and does not claim compliance with healthcare privacy regulations.
