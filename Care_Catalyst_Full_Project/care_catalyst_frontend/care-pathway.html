Care Pathway

Record symptoms and organize the details you may want to discuss with a qualified healthcare professional.

Urgent safety notice

If someone has severe difficulty breathing, severe chest pain, new facial drooping or weakness, trouble speaking, loss of consciousness, or another immediately life-threatening symptom, seek emergency medical help now. In India, call 112 for emergency assistance. Do not wait to complete this form.

Tell us what is happening

This prototype records your words; it does not diagnose or estimate disease probability.

Separate symptoms with commas, semicolons, new lines, or “and”.

What this can help with

Good notes make care conversations easier to prepare for.

1
Summarize what you noticed

Keep symptom wording, duration, and changes together.

2
Notice missing details

Consider when symptoms began, what changed, and what you want to ask.

3
Prepare for a visit

Save the note to your local timeline and include it in a visit brief.

i

This first version does not perform clinically validated combination matching. No probabilities or diagnosis are generated.
