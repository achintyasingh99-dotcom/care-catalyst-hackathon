2. Symptom-matching results

These are educational matches from a small, rule-based demonstration library. They are not diagnoses, probabilities, or a substitute for clinical assessment.

Important: Different conditions can cause similar symptoms, and serious conditions may not match a simple rule. A match does not mean that a person has that condition. A lack of matches does not mean that the person is healthy. Contact a qualified healthcare professional for assessment, especially if symptoms are new, persistent, severe, or worsening.
